BO GO VIET - HUONG DAN NHANH

1. Bo go: cua so nho hien chu V (xanh) la dang go tieng Viet, E (do) la dang tat.
   Ctrl+Shift de bat/tat. Chon Telex hoac VNI trong cua so.
   Go tat: bam "Sua go tat", moi dong dang  ko=khong
2. Cong cu tieng Viet (doi chu, so thanh chu, font): mo bieu tuong "Cong cu tieng Viet".
3. Word/Excel: mo Word hoac Excel, vao Insert > My Add-ins > Developer Add-ins,
   chon "So thanh chu". Can Internet khi mo lan dau.
4. Neu Windows hien "Windows protected your PC": bam "More info" > "Run anyway".
   (Phan mem chua co chu ky so tra phi nen Windows canh bao.)
5. Phan mem khong go duoc trong chuong trinh chay quyen Administrator:
   bam chuot phai BoGoViet > Run as administrator.
6. Go cai dat: Settings > Apps > Bo go Viet > Uninstall.
